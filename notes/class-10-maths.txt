SHARMA CLASSES - CLASS 10 MATHS REVISION NOTES

Pair of Linear Equations
- A solution is an ordered pair that satisfies both equations.
- Substitution and elimination are useful algebraic methods for finding a solution.
- Graphically, the solution is the intersection of the two lines when one unique solution exists.

Quadratic Equations
- Standard form: ax^2 + bx + c = 0, where a is not zero.
- The quadratic formula is x = (-b +/- sqrt(b^2 - 4ac)) / (2a).
- The discriminant D = b^2 - 4ac indicates the type of roots: positive gives two distinct real roots, zero gives equal real roots, and negative gives no real roots.

Arithmetic Progressions
- An arithmetic progression has a constant common difference d.
- The nth term is a_n = a + (n - 1)d.
- The sum of the first n terms is S_n = n/2 [2a + (n - 1)d].

Trigonometry
- For an acute angle in a right triangle: sin(theta) = opposite/hypotenuse, cos(theta) = adjacent/hypotenuse, tan(theta) = opposite/adjacent.
- Identity: sin^2(theta) + cos^2(theta) = 1.
- Draw a right triangle and label the sides before selecting a ratio.

Statistics and Probability
- Mean = sum of observations / number of observations.
- Probability of an event = favourable outcomes / total equally likely outcomes.
- Probability is between 0 and 1 inclusive.
