SHARMA CLASSES - CLASS 10 SCIENCE REVISION NOTES

Chemical Reactions
- A chemical equation represents reactants changing into products.
- Balance an equation by changing coefficients, not the chemical formula subscripts.
- Common reaction types include combination, decomposition, displacement, and double displacement.

Acids, Bases, and Salts
- Acids produce hydrogen ions in aqueous solution; bases produce hydroxide ions.
- The pH scale indicates acidity or basicity: values below 7 are acidic, 7 is neutral, and above 7 is basic.
- Always follow classroom safety instructions when handling laboratory chemicals.

Life Processes
- Nutrition, respiration, transport, and excretion are essential life processes.
- In humans, digestion breaks food into simpler substances that can be absorbed.
- Respiration releases energy from food; breathing and cellular respiration are related but different processes.

Light
- The mirror formula is 1/f = 1/v + 1/u, using the sign convention taught in class.
- Magnification for a mirror is m = -v/u.
- Draw a ray diagram to check the position and nature of an image.

Electricity and Magnetism
- Ohm's law: V = IR, where V is potential difference, I is current, and R is resistance.
- In a series circuit, current is the same through each component; in a parallel circuit, potential difference is the same across branches.
- An electric current produces a magnetic field around its conductor.
