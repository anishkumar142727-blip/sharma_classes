SHARMA CLASSES - CLASS 9 MATHS REVISION NOTES

Number Systems
- Rational numbers can be written as p/q, where p and q are integers and q is not zero.
- A rational number has a terminating or repeating decimal expansion.
- Irrational numbers have non-terminating, non-repeating decimal expansions.
- Real numbers include both rational and irrational numbers.

Polynomials
- A polynomial in one variable is an algebraic expression with non-negative integer powers of the variable.
- The highest power with a non-zero coefficient is its degree.
- A zero of a polynomial is a value that makes the polynomial equal to zero.
- Useful identities: (a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2; (a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2; a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b).

Linear Equations in Two Variables
- An equation ax + by + c = 0 has ordered-pair solutions (x, y).
- Each solution is a point on the graph of the equation.
- The graph of a linear equation in two variables is a straight line.

Coordinate Geometry
- The horizontal axis is the x-axis and the vertical axis is the y-axis.
- Their intersection is the origin (0, 0).
- A point is written as (x, y); x is the abscissa and y is the ordinate.

Triangles
- The interior angles of a triangle add up to 180 degrees.
- Congruent triangles have the same shape and size; check a valid congruence rule before matching sides.
- In an isosceles triangle, the angles opposite the equal sides are equal.
