SHARMA CLASSES - CLASS 9 SCIENCE REVISION NOTES

Matter Around Us
- Matter is made of particles that have spaces between them and are in continuous motion.
- Diffusion is the movement of particles from a region of higher concentration to lower concentration.
- Evaporation causes cooling; it increases with surface area, temperature, and wind speed.

Atoms and Molecules
- An atom is the smallest unit of an element that takes part in a chemical reaction.
- A molecule contains two or more atoms chemically bonded together.
- Use valency to write a chemical formula, then check that the total positive and negative charges balance.

Cells and Tissues
- The cell membrane controls movement of substances into and out of the cell.
- The nucleus contains genetic material and helps control cell activities.
- A tissue is a group of similar cells that work together to perform a function.

Motion
- Speed = distance / time.
- Velocity includes direction; acceleration is the change in velocity per unit time.
- For uniform acceleration: v = u + at and s = ut + (1/2)at^2.

Force, Work, and Sound
- Force can change an object's motion or shape; the SI unit of force is the newton (N).
- Work is done when a force causes displacement in its direction: W = F x s.
- Sound is produced by vibrations and needs a medium to travel.
